# Forge Progress & Mastery Engine

Version: 2.0

---

# Vision

The Progress & Mastery Engine is the central intelligence system of Forge.

It tracks every learner interaction, evaluates mastery across all frontend engineering domains, identifies strengths and weaknesses, and continuously recommends the next best learning path.

The goal is not to measure completion.

The goal is to measure understanding.

Learners should never wonder:

"What should I learn next?"

Forge should always know.

---

# Core Philosophy

Forge measures mastery.

Not lesson completion.

Not video watch time.

Not streaks alone.

Mastery is earned through demonstrated understanding.

# Success Definition

The Progress & Mastery Engine should make every learner feel guided, supported, and continuously challenged.

Instead of simply completing courses, learners should develop genuine frontend engineering mastery through measurable progress, deliberate practice, and intelligent recommendations.

By completing the full mastery journey, learners should be confident, job-ready, and capable of succeeding as professional frontend engineers.
