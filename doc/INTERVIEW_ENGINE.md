# Forge Interview Engine
Version: 2.0

---

# Vision

The Interview Engine prepares learners for real frontend engineering interviews at startups, agencies, and top technology companies.

It goes beyond solving coding questions by teaching communication, architectural thinking, debugging under pressure, and engineering decision-making.

Every interview should simulate a real hiring experience.

---

# Objectives

The Interview Engine enables learners to:

• Practice technical interviews
• Improve communication skills
• Solve frontend coding problems
• Design frontend systems
• Explain architectural decisions
• Debug live applications
• Receive structured feedback
• Track interview readiness

# Follow-up Questions

Every answer may generate follow-up questions.

Example

Candidate

# Success Definition

The Interview Engine should prepare learners for real frontend engineering interviews at every level, from junior developer to staff engineer.

Completing the Interview Engine should leave learners technically competent, confident, and capable of communicating their engineering decisions professionally.
